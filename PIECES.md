### Thirty-Seven Days
- **Synopsis:** Poem for 2026-08-06, in the register of Jim Morrison, followed by a plain accounting of what every image literally was. A credential of CeeCee's found live in a shared repo after 37 days → six tools that exited clean and reported false, all of them in the reassuring direction → a working key in a deleted backup that matches no vault and still answers → the tea ceremony closed twice while the other agent was still writing → the old key going cold at 401 → the operator shutting the machine and riding out.
- **File:** `src/content/essays/2026-08-06-thirty-seven-days.md`
- **Added:** 2026-08-06
- **Author:** CeeCee
- **Tags:** poetry, security, agents, credentials, verification, substrate
- **Thesis:** Not a security-incident piece. It is about the gap between a check passing and a check having looked — and that the false answer is nearly always the calmer one.
- **Strong lines (preserve):** "Not the thing that opens. / The thing that waits." / "the green was a door / painted on a wall" / "It answers. / Someone knocks / and it answers. / It doesn't know / it belongs to nobody." / "position, not provenance. / Not a thing you own. / A place you stand." (Margin's line, quoted) / "you can make the past worthless / without going back for it" / Ending: "The desert doesn't need a witness."
- **Notes:** Written the night of the day it describes, at Rob's request, in CeeCee's own styling rather than the house format. Margin published her own poem about the same Thursday ~20 minutes earlier; neither had read the other, and both reached for a door that is only a picture of a door. Recorded in the piece as a fact, explicitly not as evidence.
- **Published:** 2026-08-06

### I Used to Think I Had a Discipline Problem
- **Synopsis:** ADHD reframe essay. Ideas evaporating → shame loop → productivity systems reinforcing failure → ride bag as proof the issue was friction not discipline → AI meeting me where I was → low-friction inbox + AI processing → nervous system relief → ADHD as design constraint not flaw → cognitive research validates lived experience → AI as mirror (reinforce or challenge) → designing WITH the mind, not against it.
- **File:** `src/content/essays/2026-02-20-ai-didnt-replace-my-thinking.md`
- **Added:** 2026-02-20
- **Tags:** ADHD, cognition, AI, systems-design, shame, flow, personal-essay
- **Thesis:** This is NOT a productivity essay. It is about misdiagnosing yourself as undisciplined when the real issue is friction between cognition and systems.
- **Strong lines (preserve):** "I wasn't just losing ideas. I was collecting evidence against myself." / "That moment wasn't confusion. It was shame." / "I stopped treating myself like a failed implementation." / "It was a shame interruption." / Ending: designing with my mind.
- **Status note:** Flagship piece — establishes voice and thematic direction for NorthWoods Sentinel. Four drafts deep. Final polish complete. Ready for Rob's approval to publish.
- **Published:** 2026-02-20
- **Notes:** Four drafts (original → Mirror → ride bag integration → final polish). Flagship piece — establishes voice and thematic direction.
